 Yamunotri
 The Yamunotri temple is where the Yamuna River starts its journey. It's a place full of serenity, and the warmth of its natural hot springs makes it unforgettable.
 Explore Now
 Gangotri
 The Gangotri temple, nestled in the Himalayas, is where the Ganges makes its first steps out into the world. It's a sacred retreat surrounded by mountains and fresh, clean air.
 Explore Now
 Kedarnath
 The Kedarnath temple is more than a temple. It's an opportunity to reconnect with something greater. This place is surrounded by rugged mountains, and pristine nature and invites you to reflect.
 Explore Now
 Badrinath
 The Badrinath temple is a spiritual oasis in the Himalayas where Lord Vishnu brings calmness to all who visit. It's a place of awe-inspiring scenery and crisp mountain air.
 Explore Now
 Hemkund Sahib
 The Hemkund Sahib is surrounded by peaks that are snow-kissed and feel like a hidden jewel. The stillness of the mountains, as well as the sacred lake, invites you to reflect and enjoy the peace.

 Register and Verification Process for the Yatra
 As per the Government of Uttarakhand travel policy, it is necessary to register yourself and your vehicles before you start your journey to Char Dham and Hemkund Sahib. It is also essential to verify yourself at the destinations when you reach each shrine. This free exercise is your small step towards assisting yourself and the Government Administration to provide efficient management of Yatra Monitoring and Safety.
