 How to Reach
 By Road  :  Frequent buses and shared taxi are available from Dehradun bus stand to Uttarkashi. Following roadways from dehradun to Uttarkashi :-
 via Suwakholi Mussoorie (150 Km. approx.)
 via Rishikesh (240 Km. approx.)
 via Vikasnagar Barkot (230 Km approx.)
 By Rail :   Nearest railway station is in Dehradun. Dehradun railway station is connected with almost every part of country.
 By Air  :  Nearest airport is Jollygrant airport Dehradun.
