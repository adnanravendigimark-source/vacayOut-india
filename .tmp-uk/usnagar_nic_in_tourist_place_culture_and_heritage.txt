 Dam in UdhamSinghNagar
 See More Photos 
 A dam has been constructed on river Saryu at Nanak Matta forming Nanak Sagar which adds up to the beauty of Nanakmatta . The dams of Baur, Haripura, Nanakmatta,Dhaura,Tumaria,Baigul are present in the district which have made this land extremely fertile. Only Lohia Head on river Sharda in Khatima generates electricity.
 How to Reach:
 By Air 
 PantNagar Airport is nearest airport from Delhi
 By Train 
 Kichha, Rudrapur Bajpur, Kashipur can be reached and dams are very close to these stations
 By Road 
 Khatima, Sitarganj, Nanakmatta, Kichha, Rudrapur, Gadarpur, Bajpur, Kashipur, Jaspur can be reached and dams are very close to these stations
