 Dhanaulti
 Direction 
 Dhanaulti, located amidst thick, virgin forests of Deodar, Rhododendron and Oak, has an atmosphere of perfect peace & tranquility. The long wooded slopes, relaxed outdoors, cool crossing breeze, warm and hospitable inhabitants, lovely weather and fabulous view of snow covered mountain makes it an ideal retreat for a relaxed holiday. Situated on the Mussoorie-Chamba route, Dhanaulti is 24 kms. From Mussoorie and 29 Kms. From Chamba. For accommodation, Tourist Rest House, Forest Rest House and a couple of guest houses are available.
 How to Reach:
 By Air 
 Jolly Grant Airport at Dehradun is your closest situated airport at a distance of about 82 kms. Cabs and taxis are readily available for hiring to Dhanaulti and neighboring areas.
 By Train 
 Dehradun railway station, located at the distance of 25 km from Dhanaulti, is the closest railway station. Regular trains like the Doon Express, Mussoorie express, and the Delhi to Dehradun Shatabdi and Jan Shatabdi etc are avaliable. The common connecting city is Delhi for most of the trains.
 By Road 
 Best option to reach Dhanaulti is through Mussoorie, which is the closest popular location at a distance of about 33 km. Since Dhanaulti is fairly popular among the locals, cabs and taxis, normal and luxury buses are also available.
