 How to Reach
 The district of Bageshwar is located in the northern parts of province of Uttarakhand in India. In east lies the district of Pithoragarh bordering Tibet and it’s west lies the district of Chamoli and it’s North lie the the Great Himalayas and in the South there is the district Almora.
