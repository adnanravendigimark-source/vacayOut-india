 Lake Paradise Rudrapur
 See More Photos 
 This is a green development at the center of Rudrapur near Kalyani river and is visited by many tourist through out the year.
 How to Reach:
 By Air 
 Rudrapur is well connected with New Delhi and PantNagar airport can be reached by taking flight from New Delhi
 By Train 
 Rudrapur is well connected with Delhi, Dehradoon and Lucknow by rail and Rudrapur City rail station can be reached from Delhi, Dehradoon and Lucknow
 By Road 
 Rudrapur is well connected with Delhi, Dehradoon and Lucknow by road and Rudrapur Bus Station can be reached from Delhi, Dehradoon and Lucknow
