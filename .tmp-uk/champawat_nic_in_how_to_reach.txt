 How to Reach
 Champawat District | HOW TO REACH?
 By Air:
 Pantnagar airport Udham Singh Nagar is 170Km from Champawat. Tourists can begin their journey to Champawat in bus, taxi or jeep from Pantnagar airport.
 By Train:
 Tanakpur in Champawat is the railway station 75Km from Champawat town. Tourists can begin to their destination through roadways from Tanakpur railway station.
 By Road:
 Champawat has well connected roadways with neighboring districts which connect it with rest of the Uttarakhand, rest of India and even the neighboring country Nepal through Tanakpur.
