 About District
 District Udham Singh Nagar is a District of Fertile Plains and Progressive Growth 
 District Udham Singh Nagar , a district radiating with agricultural abundance and industrial dynamism, is situated in the lush Terai region of Uttarakhand. Established in October 1995, carved from the former Nainital district, our district is named in honor of the revered freedom fighter, Shaheed Udham Singh, a symbol of courage and unwavering spirit.
 A Historical Tapestry: 
 Udham Singh Nagar district’s history is a rich mosaic, interwoven with ancient settlements, royal patronage, and a transformative post-independence era. The historical city of Kashipur stands as a testament to our region’s rich past. During the Mughal era, this land was under the watchful eye of King Rudra Chandra. The British Raj brought its own administrative structure, but it was the partition of India in 1947 that profoundly shaped our demographic landscape. The “Up Nivesh Yojana” facilitated the resettlement of refugees, creating a diverse cultural blend that enriches our community.
 The Agricultural Heart of Uttarakhand: 
 District Udham Singh Nagar is rightfully known as the “food bowl of Uttarakhand.” Our fertile plains, nourished by abundant water resources, yield a bountiful harvest of rice, wheat, sugarcane, and a variety of fruits and vegetables. This agricultural strength forms the backbone of our economy, contributing significantly to the state’s prosperity.
 Industrial and Educational Advancement: 
 Complementing our agricultural prowess is a burgeoning industrial sector. Industrial areas like SIDCUL drive economic growth, creating employment opportunities and fostering innovation. We are also proud to host the prestigious G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology in Pantnagar, a beacon of agricultural research and education.
 A Cultural Melting Pot: 
 Udham Singh Nagar district is a vibrant tapestry of diverse cultures, reflecting the various religious and linguistic communities that call Udham Singh Nagar home. Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Urdu, and Kumaoni are among the prominent languages spoken, creating a rich cultural dialogue.
 Natural Beauty and Spiritual Heritage: 
 District Udham Singh Nagar district is blessed with abundant natural resources, including numerous rivers, dams, and canals, which sustain our agricultural endeavors. Sites like the serene Nanakmatta Gurdwara.
 Administrative Commitment: 
 With Rudrapur as our district headquarters, we are committed to efficient administration and sustainable development. Our administrative framework, encompassing tehsils like Rudrapur, Kashipur, Khatima, Jaspur, Gadarpur, Kichha, and Sitarganj, is dedicated to serving our residents and fostering a prosperous future.
 Our Vision: 
 District Udham Singh Nagar is a district of opportunity, where agriculture, industry, culture, and progress converge. We are dedicated to preserving our rich heritage while embracing innovation and sustainable growth. We strive to create a thriving and harmonious community for all our residents.
