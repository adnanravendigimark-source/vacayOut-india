 Katkeshwar Mahadev
 Village – Ghasiya Mahadev
 Tehsil – Srinagar,
 Block – Khirsu,
 Srinagar District – Pauri
 Location and Access Road: – About 1 km from Srinagar, Ghasiya Mahadev village is located near the road on the Badrinath – Kedarnath road, its distance from Rishikesh is about 140 km. This temple is known by the name of Katkeshwar Mahadev.
Archaeological Importance: – This is a mythological temple, it is also mentioned in Skanda Purana, it houses the Bhoo Linga itself, it has newly built idols of Ganesha-Parvati.
